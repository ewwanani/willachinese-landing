My philosophy

Most apps treat language learning like a near-perfect grammar test you have to ace. It isn't.

Real, fluent language doesn't come from memorizing tones or sentence structures. It's built through thousands of imperfect conversations — the kind that train your ear, build your instinct, and lock the language into muscle memory.

The problem? That kind of practice has always required a real human tutor — expensive, bound by time and place, and limited by one teacher's background and range.

AI changes the equation. A partner that meets you at your level, your goals, your interests — at 2 AM, on your morning commute, anywhere. Not to replace teachers, but to give you what no human teacher can: unlimited conversations.

That's what I'm focused on building.
